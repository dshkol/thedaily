Consumer prices up 1.7% year over year in April 2025

Released: May 20, 2025

  • The Consumer Price Index rose 1.7% year over year in April 2025
  • Inflation decelerated from 2.3% in March
  • Food prices increased 3.6% year over year
  • Shelter costs rose 4.4%

The Consumer Price Index (CPI) rose 1.7% in April 2025 compared with the same month a year earlier, down from 2.3% in March. This marked the lowest inflation since March 2021, as energy prices continued to moderate.

On a monthly basis, prices increased 0.4% from March 2025.

Year-over-year inflation trend

Annual inflation has moderated significantly from its 2022 peak, settling near the Bank of Canada's 2% target in early 2025.

Prices by major component

Shelter remained the largest contributor to annual inflation at 4.4%, followed by food at 3.6%. Transportation prices declined 0.6% as gasoline costs fell.

Note to readers

The Consumer Price Index measures the rate of price change experienced by Canadian consumers. It is calculated by comparing the cost of a fixed basket of goods and services purchased by consumers over time.

The CPI is not seasonally adjusted. Month-to-month movements can reflect seasonal patterns in addition to underlying price trends.
