Employment down 33,000 in March 2025, unemployment rate rises to 6.7%

Released: April 4, 2025

  • Employment decreased by 33,000 (-0.2%) in March 2025
  • The unemployment rate rose 0.1 percentage points to 6.7%
  • Total employment rose 347,000 (+1.7%) compared with March 2024
  • Full-time employment led the decline, down 50,000 from February

Employment fell by 33,000 (-0.2%) in March 2025, reversing gains seen in January and February. The unemployment rate rose 0.1 percentage points to 6.7%, reflecting the employment decline.

Total employment stood at 21.0 million in March 2025, an increase of 347,000 (+1.7%) compared with March 2024.
